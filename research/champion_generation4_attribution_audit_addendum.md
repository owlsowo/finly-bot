The independently reproduced attribution is descriptive and post-selection. The 50/50 comparator is a **monthly rebalanced** SPY/QQQ portfolio, not an untouched buy-and-hold portfolio. The attribution supports describing the candidate as predominantly growth/technology exposure with a smaller sector-rotation component. It does not support persistent alpha, QQQ outperformance, future profitability, or statistical significance. The separately frozen Generation 4 robustness result remains failed.
